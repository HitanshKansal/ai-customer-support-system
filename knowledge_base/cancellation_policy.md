# Cancellation Policy

## Before shipment

Customers may request cancellation while an order is pending or processing. Cancellation requests are reviewed before fulfillment begins.

## After shipment

An order that has shipped cannot normally be cancelled. The customer may wait for delivery and request a return if the order is eligible under the refund policy.

## Subscription cancellation

Subscription cancellation stops future renewals. It does not automatically refund a charge that has already been processed.

## Requesting cancellation

Provide the order number and the reason for cancellation in a support ticket. Support will confirm whether the order can still be cancelled.
