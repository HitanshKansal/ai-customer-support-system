# Payment Policy

## Accepted payment methods

The service accepts major credit cards, debit cards, and supported digital wallets shown during checkout.

## Authorization and capture

An authorization may appear as a pending charge before an order is confirmed. The final charge is captured when the order is accepted for processing.

## Failed payments

For a failed payment, verify the billing details and try again with an accepted payment method. Do not submit the same payment repeatedly if a pending authorization is visible.

## Unauthorized charges

Contact the payment provider immediately for a charge you do not recognize, then open a support ticket with the transaction date and amount. Do not include the full card number.
