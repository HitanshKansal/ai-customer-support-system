# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund for eligible purchases within 30 calendar days of delivery. Digital products are eligible only when the product has not been downloaded or used.

## Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. The payment provider may take 5 to 10 business days to display the refund after approval.

## Exceptions

Items damaged during delivery, duplicate charges, and unauthorized transactions require review by the support team. A refund is not guaranteed until that review is complete.

## Requesting a refund

Open a ticket with the order number, reason for the request, and relevant evidence. Never send full card numbers or account passwords.
