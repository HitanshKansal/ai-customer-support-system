# Shipping Policy

## Processing time

Orders are normally processed within 1 to 3 business days. Processing may take longer during holidays or product launches.

## Delivery estimates

Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 7 business days after processing. Express shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days after processing.

## Tracking

A tracking link is sent after the carrier accepts the shipment. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Delayed packages

If tracking has not changed for 5 business days, open a support ticket with the order number and tracking information.
