# Frequently Asked Questions

## What information can support provide?

Support can help with order status, payment status, shipping updates, cancellations, refunds, and account questions.

## How do I contact support?

Create a support ticket with your order number, a short subject, and a clear description of the issue. Do not include passwords or full payment card numbers.

## What should I include for an order question?

Include the order number and the email address used for the order. Support may request additional verification before discussing account-specific details.

## When is support available?

Support tickets are reviewed during business hours. Urgent payment or account-access issues should be marked high priority.
